Homelab

My self-hosted infrastructure and services running on a Synology NAS.

  • Hardware

    Synology DS224+
    • CPU: Intel Celeron J4125 (4 cores, 2GHz)
    • RAM: 18 GB (upgraded from stock 2 GB)
    • Storage: 8 TB

  • Services

    Jellyfin - A free and open-source media system that allows you to stream your own media content, offering a self-hosted alternative to services like Netflix.

    Immich - A high performance self-hosted photo and video management solution, offering a self-hosted alternative to Google Photos.

    Syncthing - A continuous file synchronisation program that synchronises files between two or more computers in real time.

    Tailscale - A zero config VPN for secure networks.

    Glance - A self-hosted dashboard that puts all your feeds in one place.

    BentoPDF - A simple yet powerful PDF management tool.

    Home Assistant - An open-source home automation platform that puts local control and privacy first.

    yarr - Yet another RSS reader. A lightweight, self-hosted feed aggregator that runs as a single binary with an embedded SQLite database.

    Navidrome - A self-hosted music streaming server compatible with Subsonic clients. Like a personal Spotify for your own music collection.

    Sure - A self-hosted personal finance app for managing your money, tracking investments, and building wealth.

    Dawarich - A self-hostable location history tracker and alternative to Google Timeline, with maps, trips, and statistics.

    AdGuard Home - A network-wide software for blocking ads and tracking. Acts as a DNS server to protect all devices on your network.

    Grimmory - A self-hosted digital library for ebooks, comics, and audiobooks with smart shelves, metadata lookup, a built-in reader, and OPDS support.

    Dockhand - A modern Docker management UI with real-time container management, Compose stack orchestration, and multi-environment support.
